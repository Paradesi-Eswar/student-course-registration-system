✨ System Highlights & Academic Workflow

For Students

  • Create personal academic accounts with unique Roll Number.
  • Explore departmental curriculum filtered by semester.
  • Instant duplicate registration guard.
  • Track registered courses, total credits, and enrollment status.
  • View detailed student academic profile.

For Administrators

  • Full CRUD capabilities on college course offerings.
  • Inspect complete list of registered students.
  • Filter and view students enrolled in specific courses.
  • Manage registration statuses (Approved, Registered, Pending).
  • Referential integrity maintained across all academic records.
